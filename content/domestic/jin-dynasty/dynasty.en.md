# Jin Dynasty (Jurchen) Overview (1115–1234)
The Jurchen Jin dynasty (1115–1234), styled Great Jin, was founded by Wanyan Aguda 完颜阿骨打 (1068–1123), who proclaimed himself emperor in 1115 with Huining as capital. Allied with Song against Liao, Jin destroyed Liao in 1125 and then destroyed Northern Song in the Jingkang 靖康 disaster of 1127, carrying Emperors Huizong and Qinzong north; troop and captive figures vary widely and are treated as disputed.
Jin moved its central capital to Zhongdu and confronted Southern Song across the Huai–Dasanguan line. Prince Hailing Wanyan Liang 完颜亮 (circa 1122–1161, disputed) invaded the south and was defeated at Caishi and killed; Shizong Wanyan Yong 完颜雍 and Zhangzong Wanyan Jing 完颜璟 administered through parallel Jurchen guard divisions and county offices in Chinese style. After the Kaixi northern campaign, Song and Jin made peace again.
Mongol invasion from 1211 captured Zhongdu in 1215 and forced Jin to move to Bianjing; the main army was destroyed at Sanfeng Mountain in 1232, and Mongol–Song forces extinguished Jin at Caizhou in 1234, where Aizong killed himself and Modi Chenglin 承麟 died. The transmitted line counts ten emperors. Territory is schematic only.
